## The boundary that matters here

A channel playbook says how the **medium** works; it never overrides the account's copy
doctrine, suppression ledger, or send authority. Where `cold-email.md` and the upstream PVP
standard pull in different directions, that is C1 — parallel by rule, account's choice,
never blended in one campaign.

## Revision rule

Same as the shelf (`playbooks/CONTEXT.md`). Channel facts that are really platform facts
(deliverability constants, connection limits) may be updated freely with a
verify-current-guidance caveat — platforms move faster than lineages.
