# Buyer-group mapping

**Activate when:** the motion involves a buying committee — more than one person must agree
before the deal closes.

**Method:** for each account, map every person who can say yes, no, or slow-down. Slots:
**economic buyer** (owns the budget), **champion** (sells it internally), **user** (lives with
the result), **blocker** (threatened by the change), **influencer** (consulted, no vote). For
each: name, role, what they measure themselves on, what the change costs *them*, and their best
channel.

**Output:** one table per account in `accounts/<slug>/outputs/`, feeding persona files only after
a human cut. The persona template (`accounts/<slug>/context/personas/template.md`) already holds
the per-role shape.
