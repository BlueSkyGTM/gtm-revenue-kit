# Demo scripting

**Activate when:** the offer is demoable — software, a dashboard, a repeatable walkthrough that
shows value live.

**Method:** timed beats, not features. Open on the buyer's stated pain (60s), show the single
moment of obvious value early (by minute 5), one story arc per persona in the room, close on a
concrete next step. Every beat names what the audience should *feel*, not what the presenter
clicks.

**Output:** one script per offer variant, versioned in `accounts/<slug>/outputs/` and tuned after
every delivery.
