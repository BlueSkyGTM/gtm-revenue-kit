# playbooks/plays/ — contract and lineage tags

The signal-play library: fifteen recipes, each composing the kit's skills (`icp-scoring`,
`account-research`, `signal-to-sequence`) around one signal or buyer moment. The catalog
with maturity ratings is `README.md`; this file is the contract.

**Lineage: `imported`, all fifteen** (`docs/lineages.md`) — method-abstracted from
operator-curated external material, arrival 2026-08-08. The mechanisms (signals, builds,
measurement) are faithful to the source tradition; two things are this repo's rendering,
recorded as conflict **C3**:

1. **Message frames were written against the upstream PVP standard** during abstraction,
   while the source tradition leans toward the fixed-slot template
   (`workflows/tam-campaign.md`, conflict C1). When running a play, the account decides
   which copy doctrine governs — the play's frame section is a PVP rendering, not a claim
   that PVP is the only frame the play supports.
2. **Every numeric threshold was delegated** to the account's `context/scoring-model.md`,
   where the source tradition often carried specific values. That is core law
   (`docs/isolation.md` §2), not a doctrine choice.

## How a play activates

A play is inert until an account adopts it: add the play's signal to that account's
`context/signal-library.md` (category, source, detection, decay as the play's signal
section describes), add its point value to `context/scoring-model.md` §3, and route it in
the account's sequences. Core never activates a play for anyone.

## Honesty tags carried in the files

- `02` (TAM tiering) is infrastructure, `13` (executive outreach) is a channel — both say
  so in their first section rather than posing as event-signal plays.
- `06` (one-to-one ABM) has thin source in messaging/measurement; those sections carry
  "needs operator input" markers instead of padding. The README's maturity column tracks
  all three.

## Revision rule

Same as the shelf (`playbooks/CONTEXT.md`): edits within the imported lineage are free;
re-rendering frames toward the source's fixed-slot doctrine — or further toward PVP — is a
C1/C3 matter and gets a dated entry in `docs/lineages.md` first. A sixteenth play from a
new source lands with its own lineage tag and arrival date.
