## Findings from the research pass

### The launch article recovers five whys **[V]**

`_archive/upstream/ARTICLE.md` — the authors' own launch essay, in this repo since the
clone — was re-read in full. It carries the reasoning we thought was lost:

1. **Signal decay:** *"most teams don't decay their signals, which means their 'active
   list' quietly fills with accounts that were relevant six months ago and aren't
   anymore. Decay forces the list to reflect actual urgency, not historical interest."*
   The scoring model's decay mechanism now has its author's why.
2. **Combination bonuses:** a Series B plus a RevOps hire in the same week is not the
   sum of two scores — *"the combination tells you something neither signal tells you
   individually: budget exists AND someone is actively rebuilding."*
3. **The PVP gate, in the authors' own words:** *"remove your CTA and ask whether the
   message still has value... If it's pointless without the ask, it's a pitch — rewrite
   it. This single filter eliminates most of what makes outbound bad."* The first-touch
   instrument in `foundations/pvp.md` now has its source's stated rationale —
   relevant to `experiments/001` (it does not settle the test; it firms up one side's
   provenance).
4. **The ICP evolution log:** *"six months of that log is more useful than the current
   definition."* A pattern worth checking against the account template.
5. **The maintenance cadence and operating order:** weekly 5-minute priorities,
   15 minutes after every campaign, 30 minutes after every win/loss, quarterly ICP
   review; and the start-up order — setup, score the current pipeline, research the
   top 10, then update the signal library with results. The weekly-update skill's why,
   and a cross-check for our own "operate first" ordering.

**What the article does not explain:** the specific point values, tier bands, and
benchmark figures the upstream kit shipped. Those remain unexplained — which supports
the reconstruction's call to keep mechanisms and move every number into the account.

**Upgrade path:** each recovered why is an upgrade candidate from *reference* to
*upstream-verified* under principle 1 — the reasoning is now known; the operator's
endorsement is the remaining step. No doctrine was edited on this finding.

**Handling applied 2026-08-14 (operator engaged the finding):** provenance attached at
the mechanisms' homes — the signal-library template (decay + combinations) and
`foundations/pvp.md` (the authors' stated rationale; PVP was already
upstream-verified). A correction to this record's own framing: the reasoning was never
thrown out — the template carried it inline all along; what was unknown until today
was *whose* reasoning it is. The retired numbers stay retired: the article does not
justify them either, which supports values-in-account. Formal status upgrade of decay,
combinations, and the evolution log to upstream-verified: the operator's one-line
endorsement, whenever he gives it.
