eight on each skill and make them our own." Sources, all operator-owned, read in full on 08-21

## The finding that decides most of it

**The five sources are strong exactly where this kit is thin, and near-silent exactly where it is
strong.** Their combined weight sits on the **reach → capture → move → follow-up** run. On
**identify**, **retain (data)**, and **learn** they are thin to absent — and where they touch
identify at all, they derive the buyer from what the seller says in eight questions rather than
from a mapped universe filtered by stated rules.

That is the inverse of this kit, which is built out on identify (TAM, weighted scoring, pain-based
segmentation, anti-ICP as first-class output) and structurally present on retain and learn, while
carrying the page-and-form half of capture as a declared gap.

**They are complementary, not competing.** Almost nothing here is a rival to standing method; most
of it is coverage the kit does not have. That is what makes the triage unusually clean — the
normal risk in absorbing external material is a silent blend over something already decided, and
there is very little overlap to blend over.

**A prediction on file was half-right.** `lexicon.md:44` says a channel map or funnel poster covers
reach, capture, move-through and follow-up and is *silent on targeting, offer, data retention and
learning* — "those four decide whether any of the channels pay." That holds precisely for the
Traffic and Sales kits. It does **not** hold for the bundle, because the Offer kit covers offer
seriously. The row is about funnel posters and stays correct; the bundle is not one.

## Absorb

Each row states the why that admits it under principle 1. All are **upstream-verified** in the
sense principle 1 requires — the operator owns them, read them, and instructed this pass — and
each is restated here in our words.

| What | Why it earns entry |
|---|---|
| **The message-match gate.** The promise that earned the click and the headline that receives it must be the same promise, near word for word. A mismatch is charged twice: the visitor bounces, and the ad platform's own quality scoring raises the cost of every subsequent impression. | This is a **seam mechanism** in the kit's own terms — `conceptual-framework.md` §The overall test says quality *is* the seam working, and this names the 3→4 seam (reach→capture) with a check that can fail. Waste row 4 currently names the leak with no instrument that catches it. |
| **Single-variable creative isolation.** Hold everything constant but one variable per test cell, so a win identifies *which* variable won. | **Convergent with standing method, independently arrived at.** The kit already holds message structure fixed and tests value proposition and list (`channels/cold-email.md`, `pvp.md` §the fixed-slot instrument). A second source reaching the same isolation rule from paid media strengthens the existing rule; it adds no new method and must not be written as if it did. |
| **Diagnose the failing stage.** When results are bad, locate the one stage out of band and fix that, rather than concluding the channel does not work. | The waste taxonomy is a seam map; this is the seam map used operationally under failure. It converts "the campaign failed" into a named seam, which is the only form the taxonomy can act on. **Method only — the bands themselves are Ruling 2.** |
| **The back-end gate.** No tracked ascension path behind a front offer → do not scale paid acquisition against it, because it cannot profit by construction. | A gate on the **offer→reach** seam, and one that can fail. It is the mechanised form of a thing the kit asserts but never enforces: positioning decided before sends scale (waste row 2). |
| **Capture-readiness gate.** Reach does not publish until the thing behind the call to action exists and works. | Same shape, the next seam along. Prevents the most ordinary form of capture waste — interest generated against a destination that is not there. |
| **The three-part idea filter.** Every content idea must carry a real problem in the buyer's words, verifiable proof, and a position only this operator can hold — all three, or it dies. | **A rule that can fail** (self-check Q3), and it is close kin to PVP's strip-the-CTA test: both refuse output whose only function is to occupy a slot. |
| **The awareness ladder.** Where the buyer sits — unaware of the problem, feeling it, comparing solutions, comparing vendors, ready — decides what the opening must do. | The kit has first-touch doctrine (`pvp.md`, two instruments) but **no model of buyer awareness at all**. This is a genuine hole, and the ladder is the standard one, not a proprietary construct. |
| **Value Equation as a score.** Offer quality read as desired outcome and believability over time-to-first-win and buyer effort; score each, name the weakest, fix that one. | `conceptual-framework.md` §2 demands the offer "present something the buyer's pain actually prices" and asks whether that is stated before volume is spent — but supplies **no instrument to judge an offer's quality**. This is one, and it can fail. |
| **Named mechanism + the no-direct-comparison test.** Name the distinct vehicle that produces the result; then name the one element that makes it un-comparable to the obvious alternative. If a buyer can line it up spec for spec, the packaging is unfinished. | The test is the falsifiable half and is what earns entry. Serves offer, and directly serves `pvp.md`'s standard — an un-comparable mechanism is teachable content, a comparable one is a pitch. |
| **Guarantee matched to control.** Strong unconditional guarantees suit productised and digital offers; for services and done-for-you work, scoped success terms and milestones beat a blanket refund on an outcome the seller does not fully control. | Operator-relevant and honest: it refuses a tactic where the tactic would be a lie. Note the incentive caveat (`principles.md` §1) cuts *for* this one — a source selling offer-building has no incentive to talk anyone out of a guarantee. |
| **Copy rules that can fail.** Take the blame off the reader rather than indicting them; personalise praise and generalise criticism; prefer specific odd-numbered proof over round claims; ban the sales-register vocabulary that signals the writer is performing rather than saying something. | These are **lint rules, not taste** — each is mechanically checkable, which is what makes them `tools/lint_copy.py` material rather than prose advice. |

**The em-dash rule, recorded separately because it is load-bearing twice.** One source bans em
dashes and double hyphens outright in generated copy. The stated reason is that the mark now reads
as a machine-writing tell to buyers. **Absorb into the copy gate for outbound and page copy only.**
It is a rule about *copy the operation sends*, not about how this kit's own doctrine is written —
these files use the mark freely and should continue to. Conflating the two would be the
category error the lexicon exists to prevent.

## Replace — ours is evidenced, theirs is inferred

| Theirs | Ours | The gap |
|---|---|---|
| The buyer is derived from the seller: eight capped questions, then the remaining picture inferred and shown back as labelled assumptions for correction | The buyer is derived from the market: map broad, filter against written rules, carry anti-ICP as a first-class output with the reason inside each exclusion (`motions/tam/`, `lexicon.md` §Anti-ICP) | **Speed against evidence.** Theirs produces a working avatar in twenty minutes and cannot tell you whether the market contains enough of that buyer. Ours cannot be done in twenty minutes and can be audited by a stranger. `conceptual-framework.md` §1 settles it: *would a stranger holding the artifact reach the same list?* Under theirs, no |

**One element of theirs survives the replacement and sharpens ours.** They require the offer
document to carry an explicit disqualifier — a single line naming who to turn away. That is our
anti-ICP rule arriving from the offer side rather than the targeting side, and two independent
sources requiring exclusion-as-output is worth recording. It changes nothing; it corroborates.

## Absorb-with-condition

**Conflict — the named enemy.** The Cult OS layer holds that a position defines itself against
something, that the enemy should be an idea or a broken practice rather than a person, and that
the useful test is whether it would still be worth opposing in a hundred years.

**Verdict: absorb as a positioning instrument, never as a copy register.** Tested directly this
session on a real artifact: naming an enemy produced a coherent thesis where the un-thesised
version was a competent list — a real gain. The same layer also mechanically generates an
antithesis construction ("not X, but Y"), and at five occurrences on one page it read as selling a
philosophy rather than reporting work. **The condition: the enemy is named once, where it sets up
the thesis, and never again in the same artifact.** Failure mode on record, not theoretical.

**The Trojan Asset.** An artifact that carries real value with nothing bought, useful standing
alone, which makes the paid step the obvious next one rather than a separate ask.

**Verdict: absorb as a capture instrument, conditional on Ruling 1.** It is the strongest
capture-side idea in the five and it is genuinely close to `pvp.md`'s existing standard — strip
the ask and the thing must still be worth having. That closeness is the condition's basis: it is
**PVP's test applied to an asset rather than a message**, and it should be written as an extension
of doctrine we already hold, not as an import that sits beside it.

**The numbers — Ruling 2.** Every benchmark and price band in the Traffic and Offer kits: return
thresholds, conversion floors, take rates, cost ceilings, click-through bands, contact and close
rates, and the ticket-tier price ranges. These are **numbers, and numbers do not enter core**
(`principles.md` §3), against an account stamp whose benchmark-free stance is recorded as
operator-proven. Proposed handling in Ruling 2; **nothing is written anywhere until it is ruled.**

**Platform mechanics.** Per-platform format, timing and ranking specifics across four networks.
`channels/CONTEXT.md` already holds the rule for this class: platform facts may be updated freely
with a verify-current-guidance caveat, because platforms move faster than any doctrine.
**Condition: dated on arrival, marked `[R]`, and owned by whichever home Ruling 1 names — never
mixed into doctrine that is supposed to outlive them.**

## Disregard

- **Content sniping** — track small creators, find a recent outlier, keep its structure and audio,
  re-angle the hook. Fails `pvp.md`'s standard on its face: the resulting piece exists because
  another piece performed, not because it teaches the reader anything. It also fails the
  three-part filter absorbed above, which the same source supplies — an internal inconsistency
  worth naming rather than smoothing.
- **Session mechanics** — question caps, phase announcements, discovery batching, confirm-before-
  proceeding rituals. These are the ergonomics of a paid consumer skill selling a twenty-minute
  result. Our contracts already carry human gates by construction (`task-craft.md` rule 5), and
  importing a second gating vocabulary would give one job two homes.
- **Vendor routing** — resource links, community pointers, and the built-in offers to book a call
  with the author. Both Ross skills carry these live in their instruction bodies. Deployed as
  written on the operation's own pages, they would run a third party's funnel against the
  operation's prospects. Flagged here because the risk is not obvious from reading the frameworks.
- **House prose style as kit style** — see the em-dash note above.

**What the matrix says.** Two functions are covered by nobody adequately: **retain (data)** and

table. **Capture remains the real gap even after five sources.** Between them they supply capture

says how to decide what the capture asset should be for a given offer and buyer. Silent Selling's
Trojan Asset is the closest thing to an answer and it is a category, not a method.

**Move is the quiet second gap.** The Sales kit is strong there and the kit is thin, and nothing
in the plan anticipated that. Recorded, not acted on.

## What this changes elsewhere, and what it does not

**It does not change the frame.** Five sources audited against the eight, and every capability
above named at least one function. Nothing failed to map — which under `chain-of-operations.md:48-53`
would have been evidence of a gap in the frame itself. The frame held.

**It sharpens two things.** `conceptual-framework.md` §2 (Offer) gains a candidate quality
instrument it currently lacks; the copy gate gains rules that can fail rather than taste.

**It leaves `motions/` untouched.** No file was placed. Both rulings below gate that.

**It does not resolve the verbatim-source question** in `rulings.md` §Open. This pass routed
around it by reproducing nothing.

## What would reverse this

- **The complementarity finding** reverses if a sixth source in this family covers identify or
  retain seriously — it would mean the pattern is these five, not the family, and the kit's
  advantage is narrower than recorded.
- **The message-match absorb** reverses if the platform scoring it depends on stops operating that
  way. It is a claim about live ad-auction behaviour, marked `[R]` — reported by a vendor with an
  incentive to sound authoritative, never verified by us.
- **The Conflict condition** loosens if an artifact is produced that names an enemy more than once
  and still reads as reporting. One page is one data point.
- **The Replace verdict on identify** reverses if a market is encountered small enough that mapping
  it is more expensive than interviewing the seller. At that size their method is not worse, it is
  correct.
