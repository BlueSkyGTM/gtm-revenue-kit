## Value proposition

> For small business owners on QuickBooks Online who cannot tell whether their books are right, Fenton Bookkeeping is the independent practice where one certified accountant fixes the file and keeps it right every month, unlike QuickBooks Live, which comes from the same company raising the price and running the AI.

One sentence. No em dash, no semicolon (see Punctuation note below).

---

## Pillar 1 — One person answers for your numbers

*The skeptic must believe: there is a real, named, qualified human accountable for this file.*

- QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor with 30 years inside small business books, payroll, reconciliations, and audit support.
- The same practice on your account every month, against QuickBooks Live customers reporting a different bookkeeper most months and updates running 4 to 5 months behind.
- Bench shut down in December 2024 and cut 12,000 businesses off from their own records, and post relaunch holds a D- BBB rating. A platform can disappear. A named person cannot.
- **[PROOF GAP]** No customer outcome. Zero reference customers today. First conversion fills this slot.

## Pillar 2 — A flat fee instead of a bill that keeps climbing

*The skeptic must believe: this is not one more escalating subscription stacked on the last one.*

- Intuit raised QuickBooks Online prices again effective August 1, 2026: Plus from $115 to $140 a month, Advanced from $275 to $340, Essentials from $75 to $85 (verified 2026-08-03; each account reprices at its own renewal).
- The increase lands on the software, not on the bookkeeping, so the subscription climbs while the help bought with it does not grow.
- QuickBooks Live Full Service runs $300 to $700 a month on top of that subscription, and typically requires a $500 to $800 cleanup before service starts. One documented case ran $1,500.
- **[DEFERRED]** Fenton's own numbers are not published. See `context/pricing-strategy.md`; the discovery question sets the quote.

## Pillar 3 — No automation changing your books

*The skeptic must believe: the thing that has been quietly moving entries will stop.*

- Owners have posted through 2026 about Intuit Assist recategorizing transactions, marking unpaid invoices as paid, and producing sales tax reports they call unreliable, with no straightforward way to switch it off.
- Intuit Assist runs inside the same QuickBooks file a QuickBooks Live bookkeeper works in, so paying Intuit for help does not take Intuit's automation out of the file.
- Work happens inside the customer's existing QBO file. No migration, no new software, no workflow change. The switch is who does the work, not what they run on.

# Migration offer track (added 2026-07-27)

*IMPACT steps 4–6 for the second offer on this beachhead (`playbooks/impact-positioning.md`).
**Fenced on purpose.** Everything above is the bookkeeping offer, whose mechanism is "nothing
changes except who does the work." This track's mechanism is the opposite: it moves the client
to another platform. Same people, same trigger, two different first transactions — they must
never appear in one message. Alternatives: `competitor-radar.md` → Migration offer track.
Eligibility and scoring: `scoring-model.md` §2a/§4a. Everything here is **draft until the
principal approves the test**; nothing has been sent.*

## Inputs (what differs from the bookkeeping track)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | The same Bounce accounts, narrowed: on an affected plan (Essentials / Plus / Advanced) **and their first bill at the new price has already landed** |
| **Instead of** | Staying on QBO and absorbing the increase · DIY-migrating with the free tooling · hiring a Xero-side firm · switching to a cheaper product class |
| **Differentiator** | Knows the platform they are leaving from the inside, and hands them an independent verification the conversion vendor cannot give them |
| **Category** | Guided platform exit, with the books kept afterward |

## Value proposition (the only-statement)

> For a business done with QuickBooks price increases, Fenton is the guide that knows the system
> you are leaving from the inside, hands you independent proof the move landed correctly, and
> can keep your books afterward, unlike the free conversion tools, which move your data and
> leave you to find out at month-end whether it is right.

## Pillar M1 — The exit is already paid for

*The skeptic must believe: leaving is cheaper and easier than they assumed.*

- Xero subsidizes the conversion. Q2X (its preferred US partner since March 2026) carries full
  transaction history at no charge; Jet Convert carries current and prior fiscal year free.
- Destination dollars, both platforms, on their own invoice — Xero Early $25, Growing $55,
  Established $90, **unlimited users on every plan**, against QBO's caps (Simple Start 1,
  Essentials 3, Plus 5, Advanced 25).
- A ten-person team is forced onto QBO Advanced ($340/mo) while the same team fits Xero Growing
  ($55/mo). That gap is structural, not promotional.
- **Never a percentage, never a deadline.** The increase already landed on their bill; that is
  the fact, and their invoice is the anchor.

## Pillar M2 — Somebody who knows what you are leaving

*The skeptic must believe: the accounting survives the move, not just the data.*

- A QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor with 30 years judges the consequences the tool cannot: how
  the chart maps, how far back history needs to go, what Live or the AI coded oddly, what
  breaks in payroll.
- **Payroll year-to-date does not transfer.** That is the single most common way a
  self-serve migration goes wrong, and it is skilled work, not a data step.
- Xero-side firms know the destination; the conversion tools know neither. Origin knowledge is
  the scarce half.
- **[PROOF GAP]** No delivered migration yet. The sandbox rehearsal comes first, the first
  client engagement fills this slot.

## Pillar M3 — Proof it landed right, and one number afterward

*The skeptic must believe: they will know it worked, and the bill stops surprising them.*

- Independent verification: trial balance and AR/AP aging pulled from both platforms and
  compared line by line, exceptions ranked by dollar size, handed over as a proof packet the
  client keeps. The verifier only reads; the precise safety posture lives in the delivery
  spec in the host repo (`fenton-bookkeeping-os` → `workspaces/books/docs/migration-delivery-spec.md`
  — a standalone script with read-only scopes, earned, not asserted). *(Independence is the claim, not exclusivity — the conversion vendors validate
  their own work; this is a second opinion from someone who is not grading their own homework.)*
- Where the client opts in, Fenton carries the Xero subscription and bills one flat monthly
  amount: **books plus software, and no software bill ever again.** With the standing clauses —
  the software line moves only with Xero's published price, and the subscription transfers back
  free on request. `[packaging pending the principal's decision]`
- **[DEFERRED]** No Fenton figures in outbound. Pricing structure: `pricing-strategy.md` →
  Migration motion pricing; every number `[UNTIMED]` until one real file is timed.

## Ladder-up check

M1 proves *the exit is already paid for*; M2 proves *knows the system you are leaving*; M3
proves *independent proof* and *keeps your books afterward*. No orphans. Note M2 and M3 both
carry proof gaps — this track is honest about being unproven, and the rehearsal is what closes
them.

## Known gaps (recorded, not papered over)

   rehearsal is timed and the first file is done.
2. **Demand is unproven.** QBO spend measures exposure to the increase, not willingness to

3. **Channel capture.** Q2X's parent runs its own bookkeeping practice — the relationship must
   be Fenton's before any hand-off (`competitor-radar.md`).

## The frame over the whole track (Addendum A §6, canonized)

**Dual capability is the asset.** A firm that *can* move a client off QuickBooks but recommends
staying is credible in a way a QuickBooks-only firm structurally cannot be — the QB-only shop
*has* to recommend staying. That is what converts Fenton from vendor to advisor, and it is the
judgment-layer product the practice is being built toward. Nothing in this track may foreclose
either door: no copy that assumes leaving is right, no scoring that trades one fit against the
other, and the diagnostic always honest enough to say *stay*.
