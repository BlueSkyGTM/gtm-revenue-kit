# Persona: Owner-Operator

**Titles:** Owner, Founder, President, Principal — small business owner directly managing their own books
**Decision role:** Champion and economic buyer in one — no separate approval step
**Found at:** Sole proprietorships and small businesses, 1–20 employees, currently on QuickBooks Online, no internal bookkeeper

---

## What They Care About

**Primary metric:** Their own time and their own peace of mind about whether the numbers are right. They don't have a "bookkeeping KPI" — they have a business to run, and bookkeeping is the thing they do late at night or over the weekend because nothing else got to it.

**Their biggest problem right now:** They're paying more for QuickBooks Online than they were a year ago, and they don't fully trust what Intuit Assist is doing to their transactions in the background. They're not confident their books are actually right — and they don't have time to double-check an AI feature they didn't ask for.

**What a good week looks like for them:** They didn't think about bookkeeping at all. Reconciliation happened, nothing looked wrong, and if their accountant or a lender asked for financials tomorrow, they'd trust what came out.

**What a bad week looks like:** They open QuickBooks to find a transaction was recoded or an invoice was marked paid that wasn't, they can't tell if it's a mistake they made or the AI made, and now they're spending an evening untangling it instead of running the business.

---

## How They Buy

**Involvement:** Sole decision-maker — initiates, evaluates, and approves in one motion
**Discovery behavior:** [inferred] Referral, direct outbound, or search when the pain becomes acute (a price hike notice, a caught error) — not actively browsing bookkeeping vendors on a normal week
**Evaluation style:** Gut-driven and fast. They're not running a formal vendor comparison — they want to know one thing: will this person actually take the problem off my plate, and can I trust them.
**Timeline:** Short. If convinced, they can decide same-call — there's no committee, no procurement process.

**Common objections:**
- "I already pay for QuickBooks, why pay for a person too?" → "You're right that you're paying twice right now — QuickBooks for the software, and your own time for the labor. This replaces the labor, not the software."
- "I don't have the budget for this" → Anchor against what the QBO price hike already cost them this year, plus what an error/cleanup would cost if it hit at tax time.
- "How do I know you're not going to disappear like Bench did?" → "That's exactly why it's me doing the work directly, not a platform — you know who you're talking to and I'm not going anywhere."

---

## How to Reach Them

**Best channel:** Direct outbound — phone or email, given the existing 1,000-lead list and short, single-decision-maker sales cycle
**Best time:** [inferred] Early morning or evening, outside normal business hours when they're not actively serving customers
**What gets their attention:** A specific, named pain point — the exact dollar amount of their QBO price increase, or a direct reference to Intuit Assist AI errors, not generic "outsource your bookkeeping" copy
**What gets ignored:** Generic small-business-services outreach; anything that sounds like a mass-blasted pitch rather than something addressed to their actual QuickBooks situation

---

## Message Framework

**Value prop for this persona:** Hand your books to one experienced person who costs less than the time you're losing to QuickBooks' price increases and AI errors — and get your evenings back.

**Proof points that resonate:**
- QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor, 30+ years of hands-on bookkeeping experience
- [inferred — capture as first customers convert] "Before/after" story from an early cleanup engagement

**Reference customers for this persona:** [None yet — capture from first conversions off the 1,000-lead list]

## Sample Outreach Hooks

Signal: Public Intuit Assist / AI Complaint
> "Saw what you posted about QuickBooks' AI changing something without asking first — that's the exact reason people end up talking to us. A human reads the numbers here; nothing gets recoded behind your back."

> "Your QuickBooks renewal's coming up in the next few weeks. Before you pay that increase again, want to see what it'd actually cost to hand the books to someone instead?"
