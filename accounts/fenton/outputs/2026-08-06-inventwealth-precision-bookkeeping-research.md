## The Verdict

**No connection found between Invent Wealth LLC and Precision Bookkeeping LLC.** After
checking both firms' sites (including footers/entity names), principals, addresses, phone
records, registry aggregators, network memberships, and cross-searches on every name
combination, there is zero evidence of shared ownership, shared personnel, shared address,
a common parent, or even a marketing referral relationship. They are ~220 miles apart
(Great Neck, NY vs Lynnfield, MA), serve different client profiles, and no page on either
side mentions the other.

**However — the exact structure hypothesized does exist, on the Precision side, with a
different CPA firm.** Precision Bookkeeping LLC is one half of a two-entity structure with
**Bucci & Associates** (CPA firm):

| Evidence | Precision Bookkeeping LLC | Bucci & Associates |
|---|---|---|
| Address | 200 Broadway **#106**, Lynnfield, MA 01940 | 200 Broadway **Suite 106**, Lynnfield, MA 01940 — identical suite |
| Phone | 781-584-**8217** | 781-584-**8218** — sequential lines, same block |
| Shared principal | Kenneth Bucci, CPA, MST — listed on Precision's team page | Kenneth J. Bucci, CPA — owner (PTIN directory, ZoomInfo) |
| Cross-listed staff | William J. Smeglin (co-owner) | Smeglin appears in Bucci & Associates' ZoomInfo employee directory |

This is the classic CPA-firm/bookkeeping-arm split: the CPA entity keeps tax and advisory
work, and a separate LLC (co-owned with a non-CPA operator, Smeglin) carries the
recurring QuickBooks bookkeeping. Same suite, same owner, sequential phone lines —
"loosely but very much connected" is precisely the right description, just anchored to
Bucci & Associates, not to Invent Wealth. If the operator's source suggested an
InventWealth–Precision link, the likely confusion is between two lookalike patterns: both
InventWealth and the Bucci/Precision pair are small east-coast family/partner shops that
pair a CPA brand with bookkeeping delivery — but they are separate businesses.

Identity check on lookalikes ruled out: Precision Bookkeeping LLC (Tulsa, OK — Nicholas
Francaviglia), precisionbooksfl.com (FL), Precision Bookkeeping & Tax Service (Norwich,
NY — different name/entity), precisionbookkeepingusa.com (DNS dead). The Lynnfield firm
is the east-coast "Precision Bookkeeping LLC."

## Competitive read (for Fenton, from `context/competitor-radar.md` frames)

- Neither firm is a competitor in the current SMB motion's sense; both belong to the
  "independent local bookkeeper / local CPA" class on the radar — quality-varies,
  referral-borne, at-capacity shops.
- The Bucci/Precision structure is a working proof of the market Fenton's future
  white-label motion assumes: CPA firms *do* split out bookkeeping delivery. A firm that
  has already built its own arm (Bucci) is a weak white-label target; a firm that is
  hiring bookkeepers instead (InventWealth, two open delivery postings) is a strong one.
- InventWealth's ~1%-of-revenue CFO pricing is a useful ceiling datapoint for premium
  bundled delivery; Precision's tier ladder (quarterly → monthly → payroll → AR/AP) is a
  clean reference for how small shops stage scope.
