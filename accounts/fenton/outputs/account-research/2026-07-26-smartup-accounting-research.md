## Why this is the warmest door

**They already refer work out, and they say so publicly.**

Every other account on this list would be asked to do something new — hand a piece of client delivery to an outsider. Smartup already does the adjacent version of it: when a client needs something outside their scope, they connect them to someone. The referral muscle exists and the trust question has already been answered in their own copy.

That changes the shape of the first conversation entirely. It is not "would you ever outsource" — it is "you already point clients at people you trust, here is one more".

**The scope boundary is drawn in public.** They do bookkeeping and QuickBooks; CPA work and payroll go elsewhere. A firm this explicit about what it does not do is a firm that has thought about capacity.
