## Company Snapshot
Wagner Portrait Group is a family-owned school and sports photography company headquartered at 11440 Olive Blvd, Suite 200, St. Louis (Creve Coeur), MO 63141. Founded in 1972/1974 (sources differ on exact year) by Hal & Mary Ellen Wagner, who converted a school bus into a mobile portrait studio to start the business. Now in its second generation, run by three of Hal and Mary Ellen's four children: Penny Wagner (VP Marketing), Heidi Wagner Sindler (COO), and Chip Wagner. A fourth sibling, Wendy Wagner Brase, works outside the business as a teacher.

Services: school portraits, senior portraits, sports/team photography, yearbooks, composites, panoramics, prom/dance photography, drone photography, and school ID/safety cards. Self-described as "the largest independent photography company in the Midwest," operating from a single hub location and serving Missouri, Illinois, Kansas, and Arkansas via a distributed sales-rep model rather than multiple studios. Public scale claims (company site): 52 years in business, 1,015 schools photographed, 450,000 students photographed annually, ~40% market share of Missouri's ~600 schools. 2020 St. Louis Business Journal Family Business Award winner. Founding member of the School Photographers of America (SPOA).
