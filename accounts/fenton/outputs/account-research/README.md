# account-research/ — one file per company

Per-account research produced by `../../skills/account-research/`. One company, one dated
file, named `YYYY-MM-DD-[company]-research.md`.

Moved here 2026-07-29 from the `outputs/` top level, where 28 of them sat in the same flat
list as strategy documents and briefings and made that folder unreadable. Nothing about the
files changed.

## What's here

- **July 12–13, 2026 — the Bounce batch** (~24 files): the first research pass over the
  QuickBooks Bounce list. SMB accounts, mostly trades, churches, and small services.
- **July 26, 2026 — the white-label pass** (~4 files): bookkeeping firms researched for the
  white-label motion, not the SMB one.

## Reading one

Each file is a snapshot **as of its date**. Signals decay (`../../context/signal-library.md`
says which are dated), and a file written in July describes a July account.

The QuickBooks price increase took effect on 2026-08-01, so it is now a past event. On
2026-08-03 the drafted outreach copy and why-now sections in 14 of the Bounce-era files
were rewritten date-neutral (past-tense increase references, prices re-verified against
`../../context/positioning.md`); each carries a "Copy refreshed 2026-08-03" line under its
title. Account facts in every file are still snapshot-dated as of the file's date.
Re-verify before quoting anything from one into a live send.

Scores and tiers are **not** authoritative here — the Airtable record is, and
`../../context/scoring-model.md` is the one scoring authority. A score written in one of
these files is what the model said that day.
