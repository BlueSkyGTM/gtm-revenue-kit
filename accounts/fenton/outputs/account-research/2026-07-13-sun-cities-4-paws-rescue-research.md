## Company Snapshot
Cat-only, no-kill animal rescue nonprofit in the Sun Cities / greater Phoenix area (main shelter at 10807 N 96th Ave, Peoria AZ; also Youngtown adoption center + thrift store, and a 20-year PetSmart adoption partner in Surprise). Founded spring 1996. EIN 86-0822208. Mission (verified, their site): "promote the physical and psychological well being of animals by providing a safe haven for cats while finding them adoptive homes or lifelong shelter." Averages roughly 1,000 cat intakes and adoptions per year and opened a new Intake & Medical Center in March 2025.

Not purely volunteer-run: verified as staff-led with paid management and veterinary staff plus volunteers. Most recent Form 990 (FY2024, filed Nov 2025) shows this is a real operating budget, not a hobby: total revenue $855,806, total expenses $1,043,424 (a roughly $188,000 deficit year), total assets about $1.07M. Salaries and wages were $501,380, about 48% of expenses. Revenue is donor-dependent: contributions $522,180 (61%), program services $318,508 (37%), and they take no government funding. Full Form 990 filer (not 990-EZ), so the books get real outside scrutiny.

## The Angle
Chosen angle: **paying-more-getting-software** (rollout-a), adapted for a nonprofit treasurer. The Aug 1 QuickBooks increase is a real, dated, attributable fact that needs no per-account complaint, and it maps directly onto a treasurer watching a deficit year where software cost competes with mission spend. The AI angle is weaker here because there is no public complaint to anchor it and asserting AI behavior about their file would break the copy rules.

**Why now:** They just closed a roughly $188K deficit year on donor money, and Intuit raised QuickBooks prices again effective August 1 (Plus now $140 a month, Advanced $340) on top of what they already pay Intuit for bookkeeping help, with the account picking up the new rate at its own renewal. For a treasurer, that increase is donor dollars going to Intuit instead of to cats.

**Why us:** One accountable QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor doing the bookkeeping and payroll, versus paying Intuit both a climbing subscription and a rotating pool for the help. A full 990 filer that lives on contributions needs books that stay clean and defensible for donors and grantors every month, which is exactly the consistency a single dedicated person provides and a rotating Live pool does not.
