## The Angle
Winning Segment A angle: **paying-more-getting-software** (the August 1 QuickBooks increase). For a contribution-funded nonprofit running a deficit, the mission-dollars-going-to-Intuit frame lands harder than the AI-miscoding frame, and it is grounded in a verified fact (their own deficit) plus a verified fact (the Aug 1 price rise). The `ai-in-the-books` angle is the strong second choice given their audited-books need, but leads with an attributed AI claim rather than their own numbers — keep it as touch-2.

**Why now:** Intuit raised QuickBooks prices again effective August 1 (Plus now $140/month, Advanced $340), on top of what they already pay Intuit for bookkeeping help, and the account picks up the new rate at its own renewal. In a shop that spent about $42,000 over revenue last year and whose income is nearly all donations and grants, a higher subscription is grant money leaving the programs.

**Why us:** One QuickBooks Certified ProAdvisor doing the bookkeeping and payroll for a flat, predictable cost, with cleanup of anything already miscoded first, then kept right every month. For an org that files a public 990 and reports to restricted-grant funders, one consistent person who knows the books beats a rotating Intuit Live pool, and a flat fee is easier to budget against a grant than a climbing subscription.
