## 1. R1 — the improvement over Michael's framework **[operator]**

**Michael's TAM is a basic system. It answers: "who can I find that would buy, and is
worthwhile to enrich?"** The obtainable market, found, enriched, ranked. That system
works, and the six stages are its honest shape.

**The improvement: the gaps we ignore are named as part of the TAM.** The anti-ICP.
Michael's system — and the field's habit generally — treats the unobtainable and the
unwanted as whatever is left off the list. Top engineers talk about reaching what is
obtainable and avoiding what is not, but the avoidance is a byproduct, unstated and
unpriced. This framework **weaponizes what is not obtainable**: the map renders both the
pursued and the deliberately excluded, as first-class output, on the same artifact.

Concretely, the TAM artifact carries:

- **The anti-ICP** — accounts rejected on *fit* grounds, defined positively, with the
  rejection reason stated (not merely a low score: a stated "we do not sell to X because
  Y").
- **The below-threshold tier nobody works** — every company in exactly one tier,
  including that one.
- **Suppression marked on the map itself** — opt-outs, existing customers, others'
  opportunities — so every downstream stage and play inherits it for free.

**Why this is not hygiene** (the two mechanisms, both already law in `foundations/`):
first, the waste rubric — the anti-ICP prevents identification waste (research spent
below threshold) and reach waste (sends to the suppressed or hopeless) *at the map*,
before any stage spends a dollar on them. Second, the boundary principle: **an exclusion
produces signal when it binds.** A suppression pass that kills 40% of a sourced list is
information about the sourcing; an anti-ICP rule that never rejects anyone is
decoration. Exclusions get debugged like scoring does — which is only possible if they
are written down as rules, on the map, rather than existing as absence.

### The stage-list verdict **[operator + second-brain]**

**The six stages survive** — mapping · refinement · find & enrich · copywriting &
testing · AI personalization · deliverability & send — as a *pipeline*, with the
negative output added where it is produced:

- **Stage 1 (mapping)** gains a named output: the anti-ICP definition alongside the ICP —
  who is out, and why, stated as rules.
- **Stage 2 (refinement)** gains exclusion hardening: the below-threshold tier drawn,
  suppression marked, the exclusion rules tested against the sourced list (did they
  bind? what did they catch?).
- Stages 3–6 inherit the negative space rather than re-deriving it.

**One naming line, so a conflation cannot propagate** (recorded operator ruling, 08-11):
TAM is the *artifact* — the map, owned by targeting. `motions/tam/` is the *pipeline*
that builds it (stages 1–2) and consumes it (stages 3–6). The six-stage list is
confirmed as the pipeline's shape; whether the folder keeps the name `tam/` or becomes
`market-led/` is the operator's call and blocks nothing.
