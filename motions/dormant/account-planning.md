# Account planning with power mapping

**Activate when:** multi-stakeholder accounts with contract sizes that justify per-account
strategy — not before.

**Method:** per target account — current state (incumbent, contract dates, known pain), power map
(see `buyer-group-mapping.md`), entry path (the warmest credible route in), expansion path (what
the second sale is), risk register (what kills the deal), and the next three moves with dates.

**Output:** one plan per account in `accounts/<slug>/outputs/`, reviewed at a fixed cadence and
retired when the account closes either way.
