# Champion identification and enablement

**Activate when:** a buyer exists who must sell the purchase internally — the person you talk to
is not the person who signs.

**Method:** identify the champion hypothesis per segment (who gains most from the change, who has
the standing to push it). Then arm them: a one-page internal case in *their* voice, the three
objections their boss will raise with answers, the number that matters to the signer, and a
timeline the champion can defend. The champion's materials are written for forwarding, not for
reading.

**Output:** champion persona → `accounts/<slug>/context/personas/` (template shape); per-deal
enablement pack → `accounts/<slug>/outputs/`, one per account.
