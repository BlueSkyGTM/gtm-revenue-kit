## The boundary that matters here

A channel playbook says how the **medium** works; it never overrides the account's copy
instrument, suppression ledger, or send authority. Where `cold-email.md` and PVP pull in
different directions, that is `experiments/001` — account's choice, never blended in one
campaign.

## Revision rule

Restoring Michael's reasoning and examples: welcome, with attribution. Channel facts that
are really platform facts (deliverability constants, connection limits) may be updated
freely with a verify-current-guidance caveat — platforms move faster than any doctrine.
